How the verification works

When the alarm fires, your camera turns on locally in your browser. Two AI models run entirely on your machine — one finds your face & mouth, the other tracks your hands. When it sees your hand bring a cup up to your mouth and hold it there for the time you set, the progress ring fills and the alarm clears itself. Nothing is uploaded, recorded, or sent anywhere.

tip Hold your glass so your hand is near your lips and stay in frame until the ring completes.
